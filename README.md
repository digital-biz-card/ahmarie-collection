# Ahmarie Collection

Interactive digital business card with editorial fashion styling, keyboard-accessible style mood tabs, Instagram/email/Facebook contact links, vCard download, sharing and a dedicated Instagram QR page.

Website: https://digital-biz-card.github.io/ahmarie-collection/

QR page: https://digital-biz-card.github.io/ahmarie-collection/qr.html

The fashion still life is AI-generated editorial style inspiration, not photographs of actual stock. Current pieces and availability should be confirmed with the business. Branding and contact details are taken from the supplied original business card and need owner confirmation. No phone number or location was supplied. The old website failed DNS resolution during review and is not used as a call to action.
